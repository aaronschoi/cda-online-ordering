# Staff sign in with a password for this app

CDA creates each Staff member before that person can sign in. Staff sign in with an email and a password for this app. That email is not a Square Customer email. If it is both, sign-in is refused. After signing in, Staff see every Order. Staff are not Square Customers.

## Considered options

Signing Staff in as Square Customers, or with the Buyer email code. Rejected.
