# Staff sign in with a password for this app

Superseded in part by ADR 0023. Staff no longer sign in with a password for this app.

CDA creates each Staff member before that person can act. Who has an email is ADR 0025. After signing in, Staff see every Order. Staff are not Square Customers. Sign-in itself is ADR 0023.

## Considered options

Signing Staff in as Square Customers, or with the Buyer email code. Rejected.
