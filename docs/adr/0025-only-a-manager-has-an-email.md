# Only a Manager has an email

A Manager has an email. Cloudflare Access uses it, so only a Manager can open the staff pages and the manager section. That email is not a Buyer's Square Customer email. If it is both, they cannot open either. Any other Staff member has a name and a PIN and no email.

A Manager can add a Staff member, set that person's PIN, change the PIN, and remove that Staff member. Removing them leaves the Staff log and does not change any Order. A Manager edits the Season, the Line, the Change cutoff, the Delivery charge, Buyer copy, and Delivery zip names. A PIN does not open that editor.

## Considered options

- Giving every Staff member an email. Rejected. A PIN names the Staff member at the computer.
- Letting a PIN edit the Season or the Line. Rejected.
- Dropping a removed Staff member out of the Staff log. Rejected.
