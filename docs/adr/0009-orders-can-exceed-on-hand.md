# Orders can exceed the units on hand

A Buyer can place an Order when its Quantity exceeds the units on hand, because stock is restocked. The full Quantity is still a Reservation in D1. The Order is Confirmed when units on hand cover that Reservation, and Unconfirmed until Staff confirm it when they do not. A restock does not confirm it. Staff do not move an Unconfirmed Order to Reserved.

## Considered options

Rejecting an Order that exceeds the units on hand. Rejected.
