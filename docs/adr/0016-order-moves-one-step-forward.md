# A Confirmed Order moves one step forward

A Confirmed Order moves from Proposed to Reserved, from Reserved to Prepared, and from Prepared to Completed. Staff do not skip a step and do not move a step backward. An Unconfirmed Order that is still Proposed stays there until Staff confirm it. An approved Change request can make a Reserved or Prepared Order Unconfirmed and leave it on that step. Staff cannot mark it Completed while it is Unconfirmed. The arrival of Pickup time or Delivery time does not move the Order. Staff may Cancel an Order from Proposed, Reserved, or Prepared, before the Buyer receives the clothing. A Completed Order stays Completed. A waiting Change request blocks Completed and leaves Reserved and Prepared available. Canceling the Order denies that request.

## Considered options

- Skipping from Proposed to Prepared, or moving a Prepared Order back to Reserved. Rejected.
- Canceling a Completed Order. Rejected.
- Letting the Pickup time or Delivery time move the Order. Rejected. Staff move it.
