# A Confirmed Order moves one step forward

A Confirmed Order moves from Proposed to Reserved, from Reserved to Prepared, and from Prepared to Completed. Staff do not skip a step and do not move a step backward. An Unconfirmed Order stays Proposed until Staff confirm it. Staff may Cancel an Order from Proposed, Reserved, or Prepared, before the Buyer receives the clothing. A Completed Order stays Completed.

## Considered options

- Skipping from Proposed to Prepared, or moving a Prepared Order back to Reserved. Rejected.
- Canceling a Completed Order. Rejected.
