# Price is fixed when a SKU is added

The Price of a SKU on an Order is fixed when that SKU is added, and the goods total is the sum of each Price times its Quantity, with no discount. A Delivery charge may be added when the Order is for Delivery and that charge is on and above zero. An approved Change request that adds a SKU uses the Price at approval. A later catalog change does not reprice SKUs already on the Order.

## Considered options

Repricing an Order when Square's catalog price changes. Rejected.
