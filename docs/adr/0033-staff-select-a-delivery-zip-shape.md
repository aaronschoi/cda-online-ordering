# Staff open a Delivery zip by selecting its shape

Staff open or close a Delivery zip by selecting its shape. That map is the Delivery zips page, and it is already shown when Staff arrive. The shape shows its name and whether the Delivery zip is open or closed. Selecting it switches that state and saves it. A PIN must be acting. With no PIN acting, the shape stays visible and selecting it does not open or close the Delivery zip. The Staff log still records the Staff member and that Delivery zip. ADR 0012 is that map. ADR 0032 keeps the Buyer off it.

## Considered options

- Opening and closing from buttons under the map. Rejected. The shape is the control.
- Opening the map from another control. Rejected. The page is the map.
- Hiding the map until a PIN is acting. Rejected. The shapes stay visible.
