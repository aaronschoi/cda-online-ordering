# Staff open a Delivery zip by selecting its shape

Staff open or close a Delivery zip by selecting its shape. That map is the Delivery zips page, and it is already shown when Staff arrive. The shape shows its name, its five-digit code, and whether the Delivery zip is open or closed. Selecting it switches that state and saves it. A PIN must be acting. With no PIN acting, the shape stays visible, and selecting it does not open or close the Delivery zip or ask for a PIN. The PIN form on the page names who is acting. The Staff log still records the Staff member and that Delivery zip. ADR 0012 is that map. ADR 0032 keeps the Buyer off it.

## Considered options

- Opening and closing from buttons under the map. Rejected. The shape is the control.
- Opening the map from another control. Rejected. The page is the map.
- Hiding the map until a PIN is acting. Rejected. The shapes stay visible.
- Showing only the neighborhood name. Rejected. The code is the Delivery zip.
- Asking for a PIN when a shape is selected. Rejected. The PIN form already on the page names who is acting.
