# Change requests until the cutoff

A Buyer may have one waiting Change request until the Change cutoff, while the Order is Proposed, Reserved, or Prepared. The request may add or remove SKUs, or change Quantities, the Pickup time, the Delivery time, the Delivery address, or the Delivery note. It can add a SKU only when its Style is on the Line at submission. A SKU already on the Order stays, with its Quantity and Price, after it leaves the Line or the catalog. It cannot switch the Order between Pickup and Delivery. Approval updates the Order, leaves at least one SKU, and keeps that time on or before the Season's end. A move to a different Delivery zip is approved only when that zip is open. A change that keeps the same zip can be approved after that zip is closed or removed from the set. Staff do not rewrite a Delivery address or a Delivery note themselves. Each changes only through an approved Change request. A change to the phone number or the Delivery note is not a move to another zip. Staff approve or deny the whole request. They do not apply one part of it and refuse another. The Buyer may withdraw a waiting request, including after the Change cutoff. Withdrawal leaves the Order unchanged. After the cutoff, the Buyer cannot submit a replacement. After a request is approved, denied, or withdrawn, the Buyer may submit another until the Change cutoff. A waiting request blocks Completed. Staff may still move the Order to Reserved and to Prepared. Canceling the Order denies the waiting request.

## Considered options

- Leaving a placed Order unchanged by the Buyer. Rejected.
- Closing Change requests when the Order becomes Prepared. Rejected. The Change cutoff closes them.
- Letting a Change request switch Pickup and Delivery. Rejected. That choice is fixed when the Order is placed.
- Dropping a SKU from an Order when it leaves the Line or the catalog. Rejected. It stays, with its Quantity and Price.
- Approving a move to a closed or removed Delivery zip. Rejected. A change that keeps the same zip can be approved after that zip is closed or removed from the set.
- Letting Staff rewrite a Delivery address. Rejected. The Buyer submits the change.
- Approving one part of a Change request and denying the rest. Rejected.
- Leaving a waiting Change request in place until Staff decide. Rejected. The Buyer may withdraw it.
- Blocking Reserved and Prepared while a Change request is waiting. Rejected. The waiting request blocks Completed.
- Closing withdrawal at the Change cutoff. Rejected. A waiting request can still be withdrawn. A new request cannot be submitted.
