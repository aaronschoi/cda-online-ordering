# A name can change, and an email cannot

A Manager can change a Staff member's name to one nobody else has, and can change any PIN. A recorded email stays until that Manager is removed. Clearing a different Manager's email makes them Staff with a name and a PIN. The Staff log keeps what was already written. Changing a name does not change who is acting.

## Considered options

- Editing a Manager's email in place. Rejected. That email is how the Manager opens the pages.
- Removing a Manager when their email is cleared. Rejected. They remain Staff, with a name and a PIN.
